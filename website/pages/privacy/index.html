What the policies cover

Security, data & advertising

These highlights help you find the right information. Read the policy for the app you use for the full details.

Information & its purpose

Photo Cleaner: photos, photo details and sorting decisions are handled on your device. Your photo library and sorting history are not uploaded. Advertising data is handled separately.

Security Antivirus Max Cleaner: its policy describes device and installed-app information, usage information and error logs used to provide and improve services.

Advertising & third parties

On-device features do not mean that an app has no network data collection. Ad services can process identifiers, IP addresses, interactions and diagnostics.

Photo Cleaner uses Google AdMob and Google’s consent service. The security app’s policy also lists Google Play services, AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. This website does not embed ads or analytics trackers.

Protection & your choices

Policies explain security measures and their limits, third-party links and how to contact us. No online transmission or storage method can guarantee absolute security.

Read about local records and deletion in Photo Cleaner’s policy, and children’s privacy and data handling in the security app’s policy. Contact us about access, correction or deletion requests.
