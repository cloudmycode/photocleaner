A simple website

This site introduces MaxDevLab apps and provides support and policy information. It does not ask you to create an account, upload photos, or submit a payment.

Effective date: October 31, 2026. This policy covers the MaxDevLab website. Each app has its own privacy policy. Contact us at {{email}}.

Information when you browse

The site itself does not include advertising scripts, analytics trackers, or nonessential cookies. Fonts, images, and scripts are served with the site, rather than loaded from third-party analytics or font services.

When a page is requested, the hosting and network providers may process technical information such as an IP address, requested URL, browser information, and request time to deliver the page, maintain security, and diagnose errors. The logs and retention period depend on the hosting configuration. Contact us with questions about the deployed site.

Support and email

The support form prepares a message in your email application. It does not send the form to a MaxDevLab server or save it in browser storage. A message is sent only if you choose to send it from your email application.

If you email us, we receive your address, your message, and any attachments you include. We use that information to respond, investigate the issue, and handle your request. Our email service provider processes correspondence as part of delivering that service. Please avoid sending passwords, identity documents, or private photos.

External links

Links to Google Play, Google privacy information, and other websites open services operated under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not embed Google Play tracking or advertising on this website.

Retention and your choices

We retain support correspondence only as needed to respond, resolve the request, and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. You may request access, correction, or deletion of information you sent to support by contacting {{email}}. We may need enough information to verify and locate your request.

There is no website account to delete. Closing the support page clears any message you have not sent, subject to your browser’s own form-restoration behavior.

Security and changes

We use reasonable measures to protect support information. No online service can promise absolute security. If the site’s data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date.
