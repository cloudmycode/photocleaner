Good to know

Before your
first swipe.

Does a downward swipe delete the photo? {{icon:plus}}

No. It adds the photo to “To remove.” You review your choices and confirm deletion separately. Once deletion is confirmed, it may be permanent.

Can I revisit a completed month? {{icon:plus}}

Yes. Completed months remain available. Open one to browse the remaining photos and continue sorting whenever you like.

Will this clear my cloud storage? {{icon:plus}}

Photo Cleaner manages photos accessible on your device. It does not promise to remove cloud backups. Any syncing or cloud deletion is controlled by your photo provider.

When do ads appear? {{icon:plus}}

After an eligible cleanup is complete and its records are updated. The first two eligible cleanups in each app process session have no interstitial ad. If an ad isn’t ready, the app moves straight to your results.
