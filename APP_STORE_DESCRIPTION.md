# Smart Cleaner — Free Up Space, Organize Photos, Find Anything

**Reclaim gigabytes of storage in minutes. Clean up duplicates, burst shots, screenshots and heavy videos, review your library month by month, and find any photo with a single sentence — all while your photos never leave your device.**

Your photo library grows every day: near-identical duplicates, burst sequences, forgotten screenshots, oversized videos, and Live Photos silently eating your storage. Smart Cleaner turns that mess into an easy, guided cleanup — and adds an AI-powered search that finds exactly the photo you're thinking of.

---

## Key Features

### 1. Quick Clean — One tap to spot everything worth removing
Smart Cleaner scans your whole library and automatically sorts the "cleanup candidates" into clear categories, each showing exactly how much space it takes:

- **Duplicate Photos** — Finds true copies of the same shot, keeps the best original, and lets you delete the rest with confidence.
- **Burst Photos** — Groups rapid-fire sequences, highlights the best pick, and clears the extras.
- **Screenshots** — Rounds up receipts, chats and web captures you no longer need.
- **Live Photo Slimming** — Removes the motion video from Live Photos while keeping a crisp still image, recovering space without losing the moment.
- **Low Quality** — Surfaces blurry and poorly-exposed shots that are safe to let go.
- **Empty Albums** — Detects and clears out albums that no longer contain any photos.

Everything you delete goes to **Recently Deleted**, so nothing is ever lost by accident.

### 2. Monthly Review — Tidy your library, one month at a time
Instead of facing thousands of photos at once, Smart Cleaner organizes your library **month by month**. A simple, focused swipe experience makes decisions effortless:

- **Swipe up to keep, swipe down to delete.**
- Photos you mark are held safely — **review first, then delete together** in one confirmed batch.
- Progress is tracked per month, so you always know how far you've come and can pick up right where you left off.

### 3. Video Cleanup & Compression — Tame your biggest storage hog
Videos are usually the largest files on your phone. Smart Cleaner helps you:

- Review large videos and screen recordings one by one before deciding.
- **Compress videos** with smart quality settings (e.g. balanced 1080p) to dramatically cut file size.
- **Keep your originals** by default — you're always in control of what gets replaced.

### 4. Smart Search — Find any photo just by describing it
Type or speak a plain sentence like *"find photos from last year in Hainan"* or *"my router login screenshot"* and Smart Cleaner finds it. It understands:

- **Scenes & subjects** — pets, food, beaches, cars, people, buildings, flowers and more.
- **Text inside your photos** — receipts, documents, ID cards, product labels, Wi-Fi passwords and other on-screen text (via on-device OCR).
- **Filters** — date ranges, locations, media type, screenshots, file size and more.

Search works in your own language and even supports **hold-to-speak voice input**.

### 5. Storage Insights
See at a glance how much space your **photos, videos and albums** occupy, so you know exactly where your storage is going — and how much you've freed up.

## Privacy First — Your Photos Never Leave Your Device

Smart Cleaner is built around a strict privacy principle: **your photos and the text inside them stay on your device.**

- All image analysis (duplicate detection, quality scoring, scene recognition, OCR) runs **locally** using Apple's on-device Vision framework.
- Smart Search matching happens **entirely on your device**. When cloud understanding is used, it only receives the short text of your query — never your photos, and never your original OCR content.
- No account required. No photo uploads. No tracking of your personal images.
